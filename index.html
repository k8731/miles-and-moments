My Journeys

Dasyueshan 大雪山國家森林遊樂區

山羌掠影、紅檜靜立，
神木與天池共寫山林的靜謐。

Muntjacs in motion, cedars in stillness,
the sacred tree and Tianchi share the quiet grace in mountain.

Dongyanshan 東眼山森林遊樂區

蕨影搖曳，林間生息，
化石記錄了時間的呼吸。

Ferns sway, forests breathe,
fossils keep the memory of time.

Baling 巴陵古道

溪谷交會，山風迴盪，
三龜戲珠靜現於雲霧之間。

Rivers entwine, winds echo through the valley,
Three Turtles Playing with a Pearl glimmers in the mist.
